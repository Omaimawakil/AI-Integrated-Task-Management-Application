How it works (for presentation)
  1. Place names are converted to coordinates (geocoding).
  2. Roads in that area are downloaded from OpenStreetMap.
  3. A weighted graph is built: intersections = nodes, road pieces = edges.
  4. Each road gets a simulated traffic level: low (x1.0 speed), moderate (x0.6), heavy (x0.3).
  5. Dijkstra #1 uses distance as the weight, which gives the shortest route.
  6. Dijkstra #2 uses travel time (distance / traffic-adjusted speed) as the weight, which gives the fastest route.
  7. Both routes are compared on distance, time and traffic mix.

Traffic levels are randomly simulated per road, so results are for demonstration only. Roads are treated as two-way, and one-way restrictions are ignored. Keep the two places within about 8 km of each other, because larger areas make the road download slow.
